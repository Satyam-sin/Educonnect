Did You Know?

Shivaji Maharaj built a permanent Maratha Navy in 1657 and sea forts like Sindhudurg — unmatched by the Mughals or Adil Shah!

1. Who Were the Marathas? Origins & Bhakti Foundation
  • ✓Homeland: Native to the Deccan plateau (Maharashtra), speaking the Marathi language.
  • ✓Early History: Ruled by the Yadava dynasty from Devagiri (13th century) before the Khilji conquest.
  • ✓Bhakti Saints: Sant Dnyaneshwar, Sant Namdev, Sant Tukaram & Sant Ramdas translated the Gita and Upanishads into Marathi, uniting society across castes.
2. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj & Swarajya
  • ✓Birth (1630): Born at Shivneri Fort to Shahji Bhonsle and Rajmata Jijabai.
  • ✓Swarajya (1646): At age 16, began capturing hill forts around Pune for sovereign self-rule.
  • ✓Pratapgad (1659): Defeated Bijapur general Afzal Khan using the Wagh Nakh.
  • ✓Agra Escape (1666): Outwitted Aurangzeb's arrest by escaping hidden in fruit baskets.
  • ✓Coronation (1674): Crowned Chhatrapati at Raigad Fort; started Rajyabhisheka Shaka era.
Sindhudurg Sea Fort & Maratha Naval Power
3. Resistance After Shivaji & Pan-Indian Empire
  • ✓Sambhaji (1680–1689): Fought Aurangzeb fearlessly; sacrificed his life in 1689 refusing to surrender.
  • ✓Rajaram & Tarabai (1689–1707): Shifted base to Gingee; Maharani Tarabai led northern counter-attacks until Aurangzeb's death in 1707.
  • ✓Peshwa Expansion: Under Bajirao I and Nanasaheb, Marathas reached Attock (1758).
  • ✓Panipat (1761) & Revival: Defeated by Ahmad Shah Abdali in 1761, yet recaptured Delhi in 1771 under Madhavrao I & Mahadji Shinde.
  • ✓Anglo-Maratha Wars: Three wars (1775–1818) ended Maratha sovereignty in 1818.
4. Ashta Pradhan Mandal & Revenue System
  • ✓Clean Rule: Abolished hereditary jagirs; paid cash salaries and rotated officials regularly.
  • ✓Chauth (25%) & Sardeshmukhi (10%): Taxes collected from neighbouring provinces in return for protection.

5. Military, Forts, Navy & Justice
  • ✓Cavalry: Bargirs (state-equipped) and Shiledars (own horses/weapons).
  • ✓Ganimi Kava: Guerrilla tactics using Sahyadri hills and rapid surprise strikes.
  • ✓300+ Forts: Called "the very essence of the state" in Ramachandrapant Amatya's Adnyapatra.
  • ✓Kanhoji Angre: Legendary Admiral who defeated European ships and enforced Maratha dastaks (passes).
6. Cultural Revival, Women Leaders & Thanjavur
  • ✓Rajya Vyavahara Kosha: Dictionary replacing Persian administrative terms with Sanskrit/Marathi words; Modi script used in records.
  • ✓Ahilyabai Holkar: Ruled Indore/Malwa 30 years; rebuilt Kashi Vishwanath & Somnath temples and promoted Maheshwari sarees.
  • ✓Thanjavur (Ekoji & Serfoji II): Built the Saraswati Mahal Library and early Devanagari/Marathi printing press.
