1. Anatomy of a Right Circular Cylinder

  • A Right Circular Cylinder has 2 identical circular bases (top lid and bottom base) and 1 smooth curved side wall at 90° to the bases.
  • Geometry Counts: 3 Faces (2 flat circular + 1 curved), 2 Curved Edges, and 0 Vertices (no sharp corners).

2. Curved Surface Area (CSA = 2πrh) — Visual Proof

Visual Proof: Curved Surface Area (2πrh)
Step 1: A complete Right Circular Cylinder with 2 flat circular lids and 1 curved side wall.
Step 1

Remove Both Circular Lids

Lateral Surface Area counts only the side wall — excluding both top and bottom circular bases.

Step 2

Cut Straight & Spread Flat

Making one straight cut down the curved wall lets it unroll flat into a clean Rectangle.

Step 3

Length × Breadth = 2πrh

Rectangle's length equals circle's circumference (2πr) and breadth equals height (h) → 2πrh.

3. Total Surface Area (TSA = 2πr(r + h))

Visual Proof: Total Surface Area (2D Net)
Click "Unfold to 2D Net" to see both circular lids (πr² + πr²) and the curved wall (2πrh) open flat together.

Closed Cylinder (Both Lids)

TSA = 2πrh + 2πr² = 2πr(r + h)

Sealed tin cans, closed oil drums, completely painted solid cylinders.

Open-Top Cylinder (1 Base Only)

Area = 2πrh + πr² = πr(2h + r)

Pen holders, open buckets, cylindrical water wells, and flower pots.

7. Famous BHU CHS Exam Traps

  • Double Height → 2× Volume, but Double Radius → 4× Volume (because r is squared!).
  • Cylinder vs Cone Ratio: Same radius & height → Volume Ratio is always 3 : 1.
  • Rolling a Paper Sheet: Rolling along the longer side produces a larger radius and a larger volume.

8. Amazing Facts & Real-World Engineering

Why LPG Tanks & Aeroplane Cabins Are Cylinders: Sharp 90° corners trap stress and crack under high pressure. Cylinders have 0 vertices, spreading pressure evenly everywhere!
Archimedes' Tombstone Theorem: A Sphere fitted tightly inside a Cylinder has exactly 2/3 the Volume AND 2/3 the Total Surface Area of the cylinder!

9. 6 Classic BHU CHS Solved Numericals

Q1. Find CSA, TSA & Volume (r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm)

CSA = 440 cm² | TSA = 748 cm² | Vol = 1540 cm³ (1.54 L)

Q2. Road Roller (d = 84 cm, L = 1 m, 750 Rolls)

r = 0.42 m → 1 Roll = 2.64 m² → 750 Rolls = 1980 m²

Q3. Milk Tank Capacity (r = 1.5 m, L = 7 m)

V = 49.5 m³ = 49,500 Litres

Q4. Volume Ratio (Radii 2:3, Heights 5:3)

(2/3)² × (5/3) = 20 : 27

Q5. Sheet 44 cm × 18 cm Rolled Along 44 cm

2πr = 44 → r = 7 cm → V = 2772 cm³

Q6. Hollow Pipe (R = 5 cm, r = 4 cm, h = 14 cm)

V = (22/7)×(25−16)×14 = 396 cm³
