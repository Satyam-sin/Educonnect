Did You Know?

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar called Article 32 (Right to Constitutional Remedies) "the very heart and soul of the Constitution"!

1. Who Is a Citizen? (Citizen vs Resident)
  • ✓Citizen: Formal legal member of a country with voting rights, free speech, and civic duties.
  • ✓Resident vs Citizen: Living in a place makes you a resident, not automatically a citizen; an Indian citizen living abroad remains an Indian citizen.
  • ✓Law: The Citizenship Act of 1955 (and 2019 Amendment) sets rules for gaining or losing citizenship.
2. Equality vs Equity & Fundamental Rights
  • ✓Equality: Everyone receives the exact same support and equal treatment before law.
  • ✓Equity: Everyone gets the fair support they actually need (e.g., wheelchair ramps, Braille books, Beti Bachao Beti Padhao).
  • ✓Rights of Nature: New Zealand's Whanganui River & Costa Rica protect nature legally; Uttarakhand HC ruled similarly in 2017 (later stayed by SC).

4. Right Against Exploitation & Childline 1098
  • ✓Child Labour Ban: Children below 14 years cannot be employed in factories, mines, or restaurants (may help safe family work only outside school hours).
  • ✓Childline 1098: National toll-free helpline (count backwards 10-9-8) to rescue children in distress.
5. Fundamental Duties (Article 51A) & Indian Concepts
  • ✓Key Duties: Respect Flag & Anthem, uphold unity, protect forts/heritage, care for environment & animals, develop scientific temper, avoid violence, protect public property, educate ages 6–14.
  • ✓Ancient Roots: Dharma (righteous duty), Rina (debts owed to parents, gurus, society & Nature), and Adhikara (earned competence).
  • ✓Two Sides of a Coin: Rights are legally enforceable; Duties are moral & civic responsibilities.
6. Fighting Discrimination & Pioneers of Inclusion
  • ✓Visible vs Invisible Discrimination: Taunts, whispering, or leaving classmates out due to looks or money is invisible discrimination — hard to prove legally.
  • ✓Savitribai & Jyotiba Phule: Pioneered girls' education in 19th-century Maharashtra despite social abuse.
  • ✓Anandibai Joshi: Fought social barriers to earn a medical degree in the USA as one of India's first women doctors.
